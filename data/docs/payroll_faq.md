# Payroll FAQ - Acme Corp (sample document)

## Salary credit
Salary is credited on the **last working day of every month** to your registered bank account.

## Payslips
Payslips are available on the Payroll portal under **My Pay > Payslips** from the 1st working day of the following month.

## Reimbursements
Submit expense claims with bills on the Payroll portal by the **25th of the month**. Approved claims are paid within 7 working days.

## Tax declaration
Submit your investment and tax-saving declarations on the portal by **31 January**. Proofs must be uploaded by 15 March.

## Bank account change
To change your bank account, submit the new details and a cancelled cheque on the Payroll portal. Changes take effect from the next payroll cycle.

## Salary queries
For any payroll discrepancy, raise a ticket on the Payroll portal within 5 working days of receiving your payslip.
