# New Employee Onboarding Guide - Acme Corp (sample document)

## Before day 1
- You will receive a welcome email with your joining time, location and the documents to bring.
- Documents: government ID, address proof, educational certificates, relieving letter from your previous employer and passport-size photos.

## Day 1
1. Report to the front desk at 9:30 AM and collect your ID card.
2. IT hands over your laptop and sets up your email and company accounts.
3. HR runs the orientation session covering company values, policies and benefits.
4. You are introduced to your team and assigned an onboarding buddy.

## First 30 / 60 / 90 days
- **30 days:** complete mandatory training (security awareness, code of conduct) and meet key stakeholders.
- **60 days:** take ownership of your first tasks with guidance from your manager.
- **90 days:** probation review conversation with your manager and HR.

## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. Leave can be taken during probation but is credited pro-rata.
