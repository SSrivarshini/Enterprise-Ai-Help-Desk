# Leave Policy - Acme Corp (sample document)

## Leave types
- **Annual leave:** 18 days per calendar year, credited on 1 January.
- **Sick leave:** 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required for 3 or more consecutive days.
- **Casual leave:** 6 days per year. Maximum 2 consecutive days at a time.
- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks. **Paternity leave:** 10 working days.

## How to apply
1. Log in to the HR portal and open **Leave > Apply Leave**.
2. Choose the leave type and dates, then submit.
3. Your reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days.

## Carry forward
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Sick and casual leave do not carry forward.

## Notice period
Annual leave of more than 3 days must be applied at least 7 days in advance, except in emergencies.
