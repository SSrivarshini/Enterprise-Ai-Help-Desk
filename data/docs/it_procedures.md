# IT Procedures - Acme Corp (sample document)

## Password reset
Use the **self-service password portal**: click "Forgot password", verify with your registered mobile number and set a new password. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and expire every 90 days.

## Two-factor authentication
2FA is mandatory for email and VPN. Install the authenticator app and scan the QR code shown on first login. If you lose your phone, raise an IT ticket for a 2FA reset.

## VPN
Download the VPN client from the IT portal, sign in with your company email and approve the 2FA prompt. VPN is required to access internal tools from outside the office.

## Laptop issues
Raise a ticket on the IT help desk portal under **Hardware > Laptop**. Critical issues (laptop will not start) are picked up within 4 working hours.

## Software requests
Request software on the IT portal under **Software > New Request**. Your manager approves it and IT installs it within 3 working days. Only approved software may be installed.

## Lost or stolen device
Report it to IT immediately so the device can be locked and wiped.
